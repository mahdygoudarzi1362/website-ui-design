# Free For Dev

Source: [ripienaar/free-for-dev](https://github.com/ripienaar/free-for-dev)

## Role in this system

A discovery reference for SaaS, PaaS, IaaS, hosting, storage, monitoring, and developer infrastructure that offers free tiers.

## Reusable rules

- Use it to discover candidate services, not as authoritative pricing or availability documentation.
- Verify current pricing, limits, regions, terms, quotas, and production suitability on the provider's official documentation before adoption.
- Consider lock-in, migration cost, privacy/security requirements, reliability, and operational complexity in addition to price.
- Do not choose infrastructure solely because a free tier exists.
- Record the selected provider and relevant limits as an explicit architecture decision.

## When to use

Use during infrastructure discovery, early prototyping, cost-sensitive MVP planning, or evaluating alternatives before purchasing production infrastructure.
